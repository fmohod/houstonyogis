Attend a Class

Reserve, get a QR, show up.

No app to download, no account to make. Find a class on the calendar, reserve your spot, and we’ll email you a QR ticket — show it at the door and you’re in. Here’s exactly how it works.

Reserving

Getting a spot, in plain terms.

How do I reserve?

Open a class on the calendar and reserve your spot with your name and email. If it’s free, that’s it — you’re done. If it’s a paid class, you check out securely by card first. Either way, your QR ticket lands in your inbox.

Do I need an account or an app?

No. There’s nothing to sign up for and nothing to install — just your email so we can send your ticket. Your ticket lives in that email.

Free, donation, or paid — what’s the difference?

Some classes are free to reserve. Some are donation-based — entry is free and you give what you like, only if you want to. Some are prepaid at a set ticket price. The class page tells you which before you reserve; a reservation just holds your spot.

Why reserve at all if a class is free?

So the teacher knows you’re coming and can plan — and so nobody has to collect money or take names at the door. Your spot is held, and you walk straight in.

Your QR ticket

The thing that gets you in.

What is a QR ticket?

It’s a little square code we email you after you reserve. At the class, you show it on your phone screen and the teacher scans it with their camera — that’s your check-in. No paper, no line.

Do I have to print it?

No — your phone screen is perfect. You can print it if you’d rather, but there’s no need.

I didn’t get my ticket, or I lost the email.

Check your spam or promotions folder first — it arrives from Houston Yogis. Still nothing? Reach out through Support and we’ll resend it. Nobody gets turned away over a missing QR code.

When plans change

Life happens — here’s what we do.

What if I can’t make it?

For a free class, just let us know so someone else can take the spot — no harm done. For a paid class, you get a full refund if you cancel at least 24 hours before it starts. After that we can’t refund automatically, but real life happens — reach out through Support and we’ll work with you (class credit, or making your spot good for another class). Missing a class you didn’t cancel isn’t held against you.

The class got cancelled — weather, or the teacher.

You’re notified and refunded in full, automatically. Nothing for you to chase. For outdoor classes the teacher makes the weather call by a set time before start.

Can I bring a friend?

Reserve for the two of you if the class page lets you pick more than one spot — otherwise it’s one ticket per person, so have your friend grab their own. Free classes are capped at two per person so spots stay open for everyone.

Safety & privacy

The part you shouldn’t have to wonder about.

Is my payment safe?

Yes. Paid classes check out through Square — the same secure processor millions of businesses use. Houston Yogis never sees or stores your card number, and the site runs no trackers.

What do you do with my email?

We use it to send your ticket and the occasional class update, with a clear way to opt out — and that’s all. We don’t sell your data, and we don’t use it for anything beyond running Houston Yogis.

At the class

Your first time, made easy.

What should I bring, and what do I do when I get there?

Wear something you can move in, bring water, and bring a mat if you have one (many teachers keep spares — the class page or listing will say). Arrive a few minutes early, have your QR ready on your phone, and the teacher takes it from there.

It’s an outdoor class and there’s no signal.

No problem. Your reservation is on the teacher’s list, so if a QR won’t scan they can check you in by name. You won’t be stuck at the gate because a park has no bars.
