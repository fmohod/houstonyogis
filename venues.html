For Studios & Venue Owners

Someone who teaches in your space is one of our guides.

Houston Yogis is a journal, a calendar and a film library for Houston's yoga and wellness community. When one of our guides teaches at your studio, your studio is part of it too. This page says plainly what that means, what we might ask of you, and what you can ask of us.

Nothing here costs you anything unless you decide you want more.

Who is asking

A publication, with a camera

Houston Yogis documents the teachers, studios and gatherings that make up wellness in this city. We publish written features, keep a public calendar of our guides' classes, and film classes and sound meditations for our Watch page. It is a publication of Cadenza Arthouse, a Houston photography and media studio.

So when a teacher tells you that a photographer would like to come to a class, this is who that is: the people who keep that teacher's page, list the classes and make the films.

What happens without you lifting a finger

Your studio is already where people are sent

We keep each guide's full schedule. If a guide teaches four classes a week across the city and one of them is in your room, that one class is listed with your studio's name and address beside it, and the button goes wherever people book it today, which is usually your own booking page.

That matters more than it sounds. Plenty of people do not pick a class by the teacher. They pick it because the place is ten minutes from home. A reader who has never heard of your studio finds it because a teacher they follow is there on Thursday.

You do not have to sign up for this, and it is free. If we have your name, address or parking details wrong, tell us and we fix it the same day.

Four ways to work with us

Take the level that suits you, including none

Each one stands by itself. Most studios start at the first and stay there, and that is a fine place to be.

Level 1Free

Named as the place

What already happens when our guide teaches in your space.

  • The guide's classes at your studio appear on our calendar
  • Your name and address are on each one
  • Bookings go where they go now
  • Nothing for you to do
Level 2Free

Let us film a class

We photograph and film our guide teaching in your room.

  • You choose the class and the time
  • One or two people, small cameras, no lights or stands in the way
  • The class becomes a film on Watch and pictures on the teacher's page
  • Your studio is credited by name, or left unnamed if you prefer
Level 3Priced with you

List your whole schedule

Not only our guide's classes: every class your studio runs, and the people who teach them.

  • Your full timetable on our calendar, kept current by us
  • A page for your studio, and one for each of your instructors
  • Keep your own booking system, or use our prepaid reservations and QR tickets
  • Send us the schedule once; we do the posting
Level 4Quoted per job

Hire the crew

You need pictures or film of your own, for your own use.

  • Photography of the space, a class, a workshop or an opening
  • A class or sound meditation filmed start to finish and cut
  • Coverage of an event you are hosting
  • Done on contract by Cadenza Arthouse; the work is yours to use

If we film in your room

What a filmed class is actually like

The questions a studio owner should ask, answered before you have to ask them.

Who decides whether it happensYou do. We come only when the studio has said yes, to a class and a time you have agreed.
Do your students knowYes. People are told before class starts that it is being filmed, and why.
A student does not want to be on cameraThey say so and they are left out. We keep a side of the room that the camera does not face, and we cut around anyone who asks afterwards.
Does it disturb the classIt should not. We work from the edges, we do not stop or direct the teacher, and nothing is staged. The class runs as it would any other day.
What gets madeOne film of the class, cut from the whole session, with the sound meditation if there is one. A handful of photographs. Sometimes a short silent clip for the teacher's page.
Where it is shownOn our Watch page and the teacher's page here. There are two full-length films on Watch now if you want to see the standard.
Your house rulesTell us and we follow them: areas that stay out of frame, signs or members' details that should not be readable, times that are off limits.
You change your mind laterTell us and the film comes down.
What it costs youNothing. We film our guides because that is what the publication does.

If you want the whole studio listed

Your tickets or ours, your choice

Listing one guide's classes is something we do for the guide. Listing a studio's entire timetable is a service to the studio, so it is a separate arrangement, agreed in writing before anything goes up. We set the price with you according to how much there is to keep current.

You already have a booking systemKeep it. Each listing's button goes to your booking page, and your members, prices and passes stay exactly where they are.
You would rather not run oneUse ours: people reserve and pay ahead through Square, get a QR ticket by email, and you get the list of who is coming. How that works, with the numbers
Your schedule changesEmail the change. We update the calendar; you do not log in to anything.
Your instructorsEach can have a page here if they want one. Nobody is listed by name without agreeing to it.
You stopFuture listings come down. There is no contract term holding you in.

Where we are right now

We are early, and we would rather say so

The calendar, the guide pages, the films, the reservations and the tickets are all built and running. The roster is small on purpose, and the audience is still being built. We will not quote you a reach number we cannot stand behind.

What a studio gets by being here early is room to be seen, and a say in how the venue side of this works while it is still being shaped.

Tell us about your space

A few lines is enough: the studio, who teaches there, and which of the four levels sounds right. If the answer is "just come film the class," say that.
