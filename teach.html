Reclaim your time

The bigger number isn't fees — it's your time

Fees are the small number. The big one is your time. The average yoga instructor's hour is worth about $30 (studios pay $25–35 a class); even at the legal minimum it's $7.25. So the admin hours you lose carry a real price.

Hours a week you spend on admin5

At an instructor's ~$30/hr, that's $150/week · $650/month · $7,800/year of your time.Even at minimum wage ($7.25) it's $36/week — and that's just the floor, not the real cost.

An hour of admin rarely costs just an hour. Stop to fix a booking link mid-morning and the whole day's momentum is gone — the real cost is the class you didn't plan, the student you didn't reach, the rest you didn't take. One admin hour can quietly eat the entire day.

Days a week admin bleeds into — even for an hour3

Count each of those as a whole day gone. Hand it off and that's about 156 full days a year back — roughly 13 a month for people, rest, and the things that matter.

When Houston Yogis handles the posting, reservations, and payments, that time comes back. And honestly — whether or not you ever work with us — we hope this makes one thing plain: your time is precious. The best thing any of us can do is invest it in people and experiences that enrich our lives: our communities, the people we love, our animals and our homes, and real rest. If this helps you do more of that, we're glad.
